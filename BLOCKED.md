# Blocked items

1. **Exact `<slug>.juno.testnet` namespace:** `juno.testnet` already exists on testnet. Nick must place its key in a gitignored file under `.near-credentials/`, set `NEAR_TESTNET_ACCOUNT=juno.testnet` and `NEAR_TESTNET_KEY_FILE=<path>` in `.env`, then run `pnpm build:contract && pnpm testnet:run`. The live deployment today is the separate factory `juno-awkorp.testnet` (see README).
2. **Email sign-in in the app:** the relayer needs `EMAIL_PROVIDER_API_KEY` (Resend), `EMAIL_FROM` (verified sender), `AUTH_SECRET`, `RELAYER_ACCOUNT`, `RELAYER_KEY_FILE` and `JUNO_FACTORY_ACCOUNT` in `.env`, and the app needs `EXPO_PUBLIC_RELAYER_URL`. Until then the email half of the sign-in sheet shows **Setup required**. **No longer blocking in-app trading on web (2026-11-10):** the sheet's **Connect NEAR wallet** (Meteor Wallet, testnet) signs and pays from the person's own account, with no relayer, on the hosted app too. Its call path is proven on testnet by `pnpm testnet:wallet-path` (3 transactions, `deployments/wallet-path-2026-11-10.json`). The QA-only signer now proves real browser buys, sells, final receipts and mounted position updates at both widths without a human wallet (`pnpm test:e2e`; FLOW-QA.md). Meteor's own sign-in/approval integration remains a manual check; the QA adapter is excluded from production. Native iOS/Android builds still sign in by email only.
3. **Live NEP-366 on testnet:** a relayed sealed bid was attempted from `testnet:seed` and refused before submission (`LackBalanceForState`): under NEP-366 the relayer fronts the delegate action's attached deposit (1 NEAR for a bid, 3 NEAR for a launch) as well as gas, and the factory account had about 0.4 NEAR free after storage. It needs a relayer account with a few spare testnet NEAR. The same flow passes on the local sandbox (`pnpm test:meta`).
4. **More testnet NEAR:** the funder file (`../engye/.near-credentials/accounts-v2-parent.json`, entries 1–3; entry 0 is engye's contract and was not touched) was drawn down to about 1 NEAR per account for this deployment. Another post, a live-open auction for a demo, or the relayer above needs more testnet NEAR (the helper faucet returned HTTP 429 earlier today).
5. **1Click any-chain buy:** set `EXPO_PUBLIC_NEAR_MAINNET_ACCOUNT` (and `NEAR_MAINNET_ACCOUNT`) to a real mainnet account. The trade sheet then shows a mainnet `dry: true` quote. No swap is ever sent; a real tiny swap needs Nick to fund that account and approve the amount.
6. **Rhea pool:** graduation liquidity stays locked in the post account's escrow (no withdrawal method). Seeding a Rhea testnet pool is not implemented or claimed.
7. **Publication and submission:** Nick fills the human fields in `HACKATHON_SUBMISSION_FORM.md`, the README's "Hackathon disclosures", publishes the repo and posts the demo before 2026-11-14 23:59 UTC.
8. **Native builds and `util`:** `expo export --platform ios` fails to resolve Node's `util` (needed by `near-api-js` 7.3.1 → `is-my-json-valid` → `generate-function`). This predates the wallet work (same failure on 8310be7). With a temporary `util` shim the iOS bundle builds and contains no wallet-selector code; the shim is not committed.

9. **Refund and dare lifecycle controls on web:** the contracts expose `claim_refund`, `submit_proof`, `vote_proof` and `finalize_dare`, but the approved app screens have no controls for them. The November 11 QA reports these branches as blocked; adding controls would change approved screens.
