# Juno 3D object kit

One object per image in the xoox.fun object style (studio light, rounded bevels, a tiny face), in Juno's own material,
colours and face. It sits beside Juno's approved art (the pastel-purple 3D characters, the polaroid posts and the lime
flame coin) and does not replace any of it. No chrome, no silver, no pill eyes.

- **Material:** glossy pastel enamel over soft vinyl-toy shapes: a wet, lacquered candy finish on chubby rounded
  forms, thick bevels and a soft subsurface glow at thin edges, like a designer art toy. No metal anywhere.
- **Palette:** main lilac `#B98AF0` (the lavender of Juno's character art), accent Juno lime `#D6FF3D` (the app's one
  action colour, `theme.colors.lime`), rim light soft pink `#FF8FD1`, face ink plum `#4C1750` (the plum of the
  characters' hats and hair). Heart pink `#FF2D6F` only on the like-heart coin (it is the app's like colour).
- **Face:** Juno's own, taken from its character art (the camera and the polaroid): two closed happy crescent eyes
  curving upward in plum, two small pink blush ovals and a tiny open smile. On the post coin, envelope, vault, dice,
  heart coin, empty cloud, success and wallet; not on the cap, trophy, warning, curve or the logo mark.
- **Background:** transparent (no ground shadow), square 1024x1024, webp at 640 px.
- **Subjects:** post coin (a coin with a polaroid face, the hero object), sealed-bid envelope (the one-minute auction),
  floor vault (the redeemable NEAR floor), dare dice (a dare carries a bond), graduation cap (a full curve graduates),
  like-heart coin, trophy (trader board), sleepy cloud (empty state), lime check badge (success), soft caution cone
  (error/warning), wallet with a key (sign-in), coins climbing a curve (the bonding curve), the Juno swoosh-and-dot as a
  logo mark candidate.
